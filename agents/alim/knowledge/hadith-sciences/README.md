# Hadith Sciences — 'Ulum al-Hadith

Notes and study materials on the methodology of Hadith verification and classification.

Topics may include:
- Classification of Hadith (Sahih, Hasan, Daif, Maudu')
- Types of Hadith (Marfu', Mauquf, Maqtu')
- Chain of narration (Isnad) analysis
- Narrator biography ('Ilm al-Rijal)
- Mustalah al-Hadith terminology
- Major Hadith collections and their compilers
- Fabricated and weak Hadith identification
