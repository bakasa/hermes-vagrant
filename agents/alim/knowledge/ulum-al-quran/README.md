# 'Ulum al-Quran — Qur'anic Sciences

Notes and study materials on the sciences related to the Qur'an.

Topics may include:
- Revelation (Wahy) — how it came
- The order of revelation vs. the compiled order
- Makki and Madani verses
- Asbab al-Nuzul
- I'jaz al-Qur'an (Inimitability)
- Qira'at (Recitations)
- Translation issues and challenges
- The preserved nature of the Qur'an
