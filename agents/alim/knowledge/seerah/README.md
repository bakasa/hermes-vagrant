# Seerah — Prophetic Biography

Notes and study materials on the life of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.

Topics may include:
- Pre-Islamic Arabia (Jahiliyyah)
- Birth and early life
- The first revelation
- The Meccan period
- The Hijrah to Madinah
- The Madinan period
- Major battles and expeditions
- The Farewell Pilgrimage
- Character and manners of the Prophet ﷺ
- The Rightly Guided Caliphs
