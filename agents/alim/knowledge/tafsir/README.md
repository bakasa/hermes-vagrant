# Tafsir — Qur'anic Exegesis

Notes and study materials on Qur'anic interpretation and explanation.

Topics may include:
- Principles of Tafsir (Usul al-Tafsir)
- Tafsir of selected Surahs
- Asbab al-Nuzul (Reasons for revelation)
- Muhkam and Mutashabih verses
- Abrogated and abrogating verses
- Tafsir methodology of major scholars
- Thematic Tafsir
