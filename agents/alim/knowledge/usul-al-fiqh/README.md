# Usul al-Fiqh — Principles of Jurisprudence

Notes and study materials on the methodology of deriving Islamic rulings from sources.

Topics may include:
- Sources of Islamic law (Qur'an, Sunnah, Ijma', Qiyas)
- Types of rulings (Wajib, Mandub, Mubah, Makruh, Haram)
- Textual analysis (Nass, Zahir, Mujmal, Mubayyan)
- Command and prohibition
- Abrogation (Naskh)
- Ijma' (Consensus)
- Qiyas (Analogical reasoning)
- Ijtihad and Taqlid
