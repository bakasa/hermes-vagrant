# Aqeedah — Islamic Creed

Notes, summaries, and study materials for Islamic creed and theology.

Topics may include:
- Tawheed (Oneness of Allah) — its three categories
- Shirk (Associating partners with Allah) — its types
- Pillars of Iman
- Angels, Books, Messengers, Last Day, Divine Decree
- Names and Attributes of Allah
- Nawaqid al-Islam (Nullifiers of Islam)
- Positions of Ahl al-Sunnah wa al-Jama'ah
